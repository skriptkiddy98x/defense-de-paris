Paris · night of the invasion · Place de la Concorde

Défense de Paris

Zombies are pouring through the streets and an alien ship hangs over the Eiffel Tower. Pick a hero, take three squadmates and hold the square wave after wave. Every cleared wave earns an upgrade.

How to survive

  • Streaks. Kills less than 3 seconds apart build a streak. At 5, 10, 20 and 35 kills your points multiply.
  • Upgrades. After every wave pick one of three upgrades. They stack, so plan a build.
  • Pickups. Enemies drop health, ammo and grenades. Rare glowing pillars are power-ups: Insta-Kill, Double Points, Rage, Max Ammo and the Nuke.
  • Bloaters. Green, swollen zombies burst when they die. Pop one inside a crowd.
